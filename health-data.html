MyMacroTrack Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice

Last updated: 23 August 2026

This notice applies to consumer health data and exists because Washington's

My Health My Data Act, and Nevada's SB370, require a separate one. It is written

for residents of those states, but everything in it is how MyMacroTrack treats

this data for everybody.

It sits alongside the main Privacy Policy, which covers

everything else.

What we treat as consumer health data

DataCollected when
Food you log, the amount, the meal and the timeYou log it
The nutrition of that foodAutomatically, with the entry
WeightYou record a weigh-in
Body measurements — waist, hips, chest, arm, thighYou record them
Height, year of birth, sexYou enter them, all optional
Activity level, training experience, training days per weekYou enter them, all optional
Goal, goal weight, goal rateYou set a goal
A photograph of a mealYou take one for an estimate

Calculated from the above: your estimated energy expenditure, calorie and

macronutrient targets, weight trend, and micronutrient totals. These are

estimates produced on your own data, not measurements of you.

How we collect it

Only from you, directly, inside the app. We do not buy health data, receive

it from data brokers, infer it from other sources, or collect it from Apple

Health, Google Health Connect, or any wearable. MyMacroTrack does not connect to

any of those.

Why we collect it

1. To show you your own diary, totals, and trends.

2. To calculate calorie and macronutrient targets from figures you provide.

3. To produce a nutrition estimate from a meal photograph, when you take one.

That is the complete list. It is not used for advertising, profiling,

scoring, research, or product analytics.

Consent

We ask for your affirmative consent before collecting any of this, on its own

screen, separate from the Terms and from creating an account. Nothing is

pre-ticked and continuing past a screen is never treated as agreement.

You can withdraw consent at any time in **Profile → Health data →

Withdraw consent. Withdrawing stops new health entries. It does not** delete

what is already there — that is account deletion, which is a separate and

irreversible choice, offered in the same place. We keep them apart deliberately

so that pausing collection cannot cost you years of history you did not ask to

lose.

Who we share it with

**We do not sell consumer health data. We have never sold it and there is no

mechanism in the app to do so.** Selling it would require your separate written

authorization, which we do not ask for and do not want.

We share it with exactly two processors, both under contract, and only as needed

to run the app:

WhoWhat they receiveWhen
SupabaseEverything stored on our serversContinuously — they host the database
AnthropicOne meal photographOnly at the moment you request an estimate

A meal photograph is passed through our server to Anthropic and is **not stored

by us** — no bucket, no table, no log line. We retain only the fact that a

request happened and what it cost.

Nothing else receives it. There is no advertising network, no analytics

provider, no crash reporter, and no tracking SDK in this app.

What never leaves your phone

  • Progress photos. Stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Your own AI provider key, if you supply one — held in the iOS Keychain or

Android Keystore.

  • Reminder settings, which your phone's operating system schedules.

Where it is stored

On servers in the United States (US West), operated by Supabase on our

behalf, protected by row-level security so the database itself enforces that you

can only read your own rows. Traffic is encrypted in transit.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. There is no automatic expiry, because a food and weight

history is more useful the longer it is. Deleting your account removes it

immediately and permanently.

Your rights

You may:

  • Access your consumer health data — Profile → Your data → Export

gives you all of it as CSV files, with no need to ask us.

  • Delete it — Profile → Your data → Delete my account. Immediate,

permanent, and there is no recovery period and no backup copy kept.

  • Withdraw consent — Profile → Health data.
  • Appeal a refusal, if we ever refuse one of these. Write to

sashreekmahav@gmail.com. We will respond within 45 days, and if we uphold

the refusal we will tell you why and how to complain to the Washington

Attorney General at

agportal.atg.wa.gov/complaint.

We will not treat you differently for exercising any of these. There is no

reduced tier, no price change, and no feature withheld as a result.

The export and deletion tools are in the app precisely so that these are things

you can do rather than things you must request.
