MyMacroTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

This describes exactly what MyMacroTrack collects, where it goes, and how to get rid

of it. It was written against the source code rather than from a template, and

every claim in it is checkable in this repository.

Who we are

MyMacroTrack is operated by SKMR Technologies LLC, based in The United States Of America.

Questions about this policy or your data: sashreekmahav@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We collect the food, weight and body data you type in, and the email address

you sign up with. That is essentially all of it.

  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind. There are no

third-party SDKs in the app that collect anything.

  • We never sell your data, and we never share it for advertising or profiling.
  • Progress photos never leave your phone.
  • Meal photos sent for AI estimation are used once and never stored.
  • You can export everything as CSV, and delete everything, from inside the app.

Profile and plan

Year of birth, sex, height, activity level, training experience, training days per

Profile picture

Optional. If you set one it is stored in private cloud storage and served through

short-lived signed links, so the file cannot be fetched by guessing its address.

Deleted when you remove it or delete your account.

payment information ever reaches our servers** — Apple and Google handle payment

entirely.

What stays on your phone and never reaches us

  • Progress photos. Copied into the app's private storage on your device and

indexed locally. They are never uploaded, are not in any backup we hold, do not

sync between devices, and are lost if you uninstall the app.

  • Your own AI provider key, if you choose to supply one. Stored in the iOS

Keychain or Android Keystore. It is never written to our database, our logs, or

anywhere else on our side. To be precise rather than flattering: the key does

pass *through* our server inside a single request on its way to the AI

provider. It is not recorded there.

  • Your login session, stored encrypted on the device.
  • Reminder settings. Reminders are scheduled by your phone's operating

system, not sent by us. We hold no device token, run no push service, and are

not told when one is delivered or opened. They are off unless you turn them on,

and they work with no connection.

Meal photos, in detail

If you photograph a meal for an estimate:

1. The image is sent to our server and immediately on to Anthropic, which

returns an estimate of the food and its nutrition.

2. The image is held only for the duration of that single request. **It is not

written to any database, storage bucket, or log file.** When the request ends

it is gone.

3. We keep only the fact that a request happened, and what it cost.

Estimates are drafts. Nothing is added to your log until you review and save it.

We do not estimate micronutrients from photographs, because a photograph does not

contain that information.

Food data you contribute

Foods you create for yourself, and corrections you make to shared food entries,

are private to your account and deleted with it.

Nutrition data cached from Open Food Facts is shared reference data, is not

attached to you, and is not deleted when you delete your account — because it

never belonged to you. It is also licensed under the Open Database License, which

obliges us to keep it open.

Who else is involved

We use as few third parties as we can. This is all of them.

they do not receive your address or identity.

Servers are located in the United States (US West). If you are outside the

United States, your data is transferred and stored there.

**There is no analytics provider, no advertising network, no crash reporter and no

tracking SDK in this app.** We are not able to sell your data to anyone, because

we do not send it to anyone who would buy it.

What we do not collect

Location. Contacts. Your device's advertising identifier. Anything from Apple

Health or Google Health Connect — MyMacroTrack does not connect to either. Browsing

or usage analytics of any kind.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. We run no automatic expiry, because a food and weight history

is more useful the longer it is.

Getting your data out

Profile → Your data → Export. Everything, as plain CSV files you can open in

any spreadsheet: your food log, weight, measurements, saved meals, recipes and

profile. No account is needed to read them.

Deleting your data

Profile → Your data → Delete my account. This is immediate and permanent:

  • Every record listed above is deleted from our database.
  • Your profile picture is deleted from storage.
  • Your sign-in account is deleted.
  • Progress photos, your stored provider key and all local data are erased from

the phone.

There is no recovery period and we keep no backup copy for ourselves. **This

cannot be undone** — export first if you want a copy.

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an App Store or Google Play

subscription. Cancel that in your store account settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export,

delete or restrict use of your data, and to object to processing. The export and

deletion tools above satisfy access, portability and erasure directly and

immediately, without needing to ask us. For anything else, write to

sashreekmahav@gmail.com.

Our lawful basis for processing, where the GDPR applies, is performance of a

contract — the data listed is what the app needs in order to be the app.

Children

MyMacroTrack is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect

data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we

will delete it.

Security

Every table holding user data is protected by row-level security, so the database

itself enforces that you can only ever read your own rows. Traffic is encrypted in

transit. Passwords are hashed and we cannot read them. The keys that could access

data across accounts exist only in server environment variables and are never

shipped inside the app.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and notify

you in the app before the change takes effect.
